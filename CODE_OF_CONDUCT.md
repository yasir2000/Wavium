# Code of Conduct

Wavium adopts a professional, technical, and respectful collaboration standard.

## Expected Behavior
- Be precise.
- Be constructive.
- Focus on the code, the spec, and the evidence.
- Treat contributors, users, and maintainers with respect.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Deliberate misinformation or bad-faith review behavior.
- Personal attacks or exclusionary language.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or revoke access to protect the health of the project community.

## Reporting
Report conduct issues to the maintainers through the project's security or maintainer contact channels.
