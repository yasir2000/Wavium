# Wavium Sponsors

Wavium is an open-source WebAssembly-native systems platform focused on secure, portable, interoperable, and verifiable software execution.

We welcome individuals, organizations, research institutions, and companies that want to support the long-term development of Wavium.

## Why Sponsor Wavium?

Sponsorship helps us build and maintain open infrastructure across:

* WebAssembly and WebAssembly Components
* WIT and language interoperability
* Wavium Runtime
* Semantic Intermediate Representation (Semantic IR)
* Formal verification and semantic verification
* Capability-based security
* Actor-based programming models
* FFI and cross-language interoperability
* Compiler and toolchain infrastructure
* Embedded and bare-metal targets
* Multi-core and heterogeneous hardware
* Developer tooling and SDKs
* Documentation, testing, benchmarking, and CI/CD
* Security research and audits

The goal is to make Wavium a sustainable, community-driven open-source project rather than a technology controlled by a single vendor.

## Sponsorship Principles

Wavium sponsorship follows these principles:

1. **Technical independence**
   Sponsorship does not grant control over Wavium's technical direction.

2. **Open development**
   Development is conducted transparently through public repositories, issues, discussions, RFCs, and community processes.

3. **Vendor neutrality**
   Wavium aims to remain useful to developers, organizations, hardware vendors, cloud providers, and research institutions across the ecosystem.

4. **No guaranteed feature development**
   Sponsorship does not guarantee implementation of a particular feature, roadmap item, or technical proposal.

5. **Community benefit**
   Funding should primarily contribute to infrastructure and development that benefits the broader Wavium ecosystem.

6. **Transparency**
   As the project matures, we intend to provide increasing transparency regarding sponsorship, funding, and major project expenditures.

## Sponsorship and Governance

Sponsors are encouraged to participate in the Wavium community.

However:

> Sponsorship does not purchase technical influence.

Technical decisions should remain governed through Wavium's public governance and RFC processes.

Sponsors may:

* Open issues
* Submit pull requests
* Participate in discussions
* Submit RFCs/WIPs
* Join working groups where eligible
* Provide technical feedback
* Propose research collaborations

Sponsors may not:

* Override maintainers
* Demand acceptance of code
* Purchase roadmap priority
* Control releases
* Obtain exclusive rights to Wavium technology
* Restrict the open-source availability of community-developed Wavium components

## Intellectual Property

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, contributions to Wavium remain subject to the project's applicable open-source licensing and contribution policies.

Sponsorship does not transfer ownership of Wavium's source code, specifications, documentation, trademarks, or other project assets.

Specific intellectual-property arrangements for separately funded research or jointly developed work should be documented independently before development begins.

## Trademark and Branding

The Wavium name, logo, and associated branding may be protected separately from the project's open-source source code.

Sponsorship does not automatically grant permission to use Wavium trademarks.

A future Wavium trademark policy will define acceptable uses of:

* Wavium
* Wavium logos
* Wavium Foundation
* Wavium Certified
* Wavium-compatible
* Wavium ecosystem branding

## Funding Transparency

As Wavium's funding grows, the project intends to develop transparent financial practices.

Future transparency may include:

* Sponsorship totals
* Major funding sources
* Infrastructure expenditure
* Development expenditure
* Security expenditure
* Research expenditure
* Community expenditure
* Annual financial summaries

Personal financial information and confidential commercial information will not be unnecessarily disclosed.

## Fiscal and Legal Structure

Wavium is currently an open-source software project.

The project may use appropriate sponsorship platforms, fiscal hosts, organizations, or other legal structures as it develops.

The creation of a formal Wavium foundation or nonprofit organization will be considered when the project's community, funding, governance, and international activities justify such a structure.

Until then, sponsorship should not be interpreted as a charitable donation unless explicitly processed through an appropriately authorized charitable organization.

## Sponsorship Does Not Guarantee Services

Wavium sponsorship is intended to support open-source development.

Unless a separate written agreement exists, sponsorship does not create an obligation to provide:

* Consulting
* Support contracts
* Service-level agreements
* Custom development
* Feature commitments
* Commercial licenses
* Priority bug fixes
* Private builds
* Exclusive access

Organizations requiring commercial services may establish separate agreements with independent Wavium ecosystem participants.
